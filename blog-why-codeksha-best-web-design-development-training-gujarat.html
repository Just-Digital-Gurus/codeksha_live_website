Why Students Choose Codeksha for Web Design & Development Training in Gujarat

December 8, 2025 5 min read Codeksha Team

Web design and development training varies a lot in format and quality. Here's a specific, honest look at what Codeksha offers, so you can judge the fit for yourself rather than take a marketing claim at face value.

A Combined Design-and-Code Curriculum

Codeksha's Frontend Design course covers both sides of web design: visual/UI principles and wireframing through Figma, and the actual implementation in HTML, CSS, JavaScript and modern frameworks - so students aren't left with only design theory or only code.

Instructors With Real Client Experience

Training is led by developers with hands-on experience building real client work, not instructors teaching purely from textbooks.

Structured, Project-Based Learning

The course curriculum runs through structured monthly modules ending in a real final project - a portfolio website or UI build - rather than an unstructured mix of topics.

On-Premise Training in Rajkot

Training takes place in person at Codeksha's Rajkot centre, which is accessible to students from across the wider Gujarat region who are willing to attend on-premise classes.

A Full Web Development Ecosystem, Not Just One Course

Beyond design, students have direct pathways into backend, CMS, and full stack courses at the same institute - useful for anyone who wants to grow beyond frontend design specifically.

If you're evaluating web design and development training options, you're welcome to visit Codeksha's Rajkot centre or book a free demo class to see the teaching approach directly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Codeksha train students from outside Rajkot?

Training is delivered on-premise at our Rajkot centre, so students need to be able to attend classes there in person.

Is prior design experience required for the Frontend Design course?

No - the course starts from HTML/CSS fundamentals and builds up to design tools and modern frameworks, so no prior design or coding background is required.
