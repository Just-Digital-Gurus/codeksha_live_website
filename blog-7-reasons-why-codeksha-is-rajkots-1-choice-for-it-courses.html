7 Reasons Students Choose Codeksha for IT Courses in Rajkot

December 6, 2025 5 min read Codeksha Team

Rather than making an unsupported claim about rankings, here are seven specific, verifiable reasons students choose Codeksha for IT training in Rajkot.

1. Instructors Are Working Developers

Codeksha's instructors are active developers at Creatiz and Just Digital Gurus who bring real client-project experience directly into the classroom, rather than teaching from a purely academic background.

2. Every Course Includes Live Project Work

Courses aren't lecture-only - each one includes hands-on, project-based assignments, so students build real, working projects rather than just absorbing theory.

3. On-Premise Classroom Training in Rajkot

Training happens in person, at Codeksha's Rajkot centre, with direct, real-time access to instructors rather than pre-recorded video content.

4. A Genuinely Broad Course Catalog

Codeksha offers 15 courses across web development, CMS tools, and digital marketing - covering Full Stack, Backend, Frontend, Laravel, PHP, React, Node.js, WordPress, Bootstrap, and more, so students can build a learning path suited to their specific goals.

5. Flexible, Student-Friendly Timings

Class timings are structured to accommodate college students and working professionals, rather than assuming everyone has an entirely free schedule.

6. A Certificate of Completion

Students who complete a course receive a certificate from Codeksha recognizing their training.

7. A Free Demo Class Before You Commit

Prospective students can attend a free demo class to experience the teaching style and classroom environment directly before enrolling - a low-risk way to evaluate fit.

If these reasons match what you're looking for in an IT training institute, you're welcome to book a free demo class or browse our course catalog.
