Privacy Policy

How Codeksha collects, uses and protects your personal information.

At Codeksha, your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you engage with our website, services, and offline training programs. By using our services, you agree to the terms outlined below.

1 About Codeksha

Codeksha is a training institute offering offline courses in web development, graphic design, UI/UX, and other tech-related fields. Unlike traditional coaching centers, we believe in practical learning - our students are trained directly by industry-expert developers and designers.

2 Information We Collect

To provide the best possible experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Full name, phone number, email address, residential address, and emergency contact.
  • Academic & Professional Background: Prior experience, education level, and skill set, to assign appropriate batches or mentors.
  • Payment Information: For processing course fees securely.
  • Website Data: Browsing activity, device information, IP address, and location data to enhance site experience.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Register you for our offline and upcoming training courses.
  • Offer personalized guidance and mentorship based on your learning goals.
  • Communicate updates about course schedules, workshops, exams, and events.
  • Improve our services and customize course offerings.
  • Ensure compliance with local laws and maintain internal records.

4 Data Sharing & Security

We value your trust and do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Any information you share is used solely for educational and administrative purposes.

We employ industry-standard security protocols to keep your data safe, including:

  • Encrypted storage systems
  • Secure payment gateways
  • Access control for sensitive data

5 Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies to improve performance and analyze traffic. These cookies help us track user interactions to deliver a better experience. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

6 Your Rights

As a user of Codeksha, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we store
  • Request corrections or updates
  • Ask us to delete your data (subject to legal compliance)

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@codeksha.com.

7 Third-Party Links

Our website or communication materials may contain links to third-party platforms such as YouTube, Google Forms, or payment processors. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and recommend reviewing their policies before engaging with them.

8 Updates to This Policy

Codeksha reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. We encourage you to review this page regularly. Continued use of our services after updates signifies your acceptance of the revised terms.
