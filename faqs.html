General Inquiries

Codeksha is an offline training center focused on providing hands-on coding and development courses. We specialize in helping students gain real-world skills through practical learning experiences with expert instructors.

Our courses are designed for individuals who are eager to learn coding, whether you're a college student, fresh graduate, or working professional. No prior experience is necessary for most courses.

Not at all! Our programs are designed to accommodate beginners. Our instructors will guide you through the foundational concepts and help you build your skills progressively.

Codeksha provides a hands-on, real-world approach to learning coding. Our instructors are active developers working in the industry, offering direct insights into the latest practices and trends.

We offer a variety of courses including Full Stack Development, Frontend Development, Backend Programming, Web Development with WordPress, PHP, Laravel, and much more. See our full course catalog.

Simply visit our center to register, or contact us online to schedule a consultation. We'll help you choose the best course that fits your learning goals.

Yes, we offer flexible installment payment options for certain courses. Please inquire at our center for more details.

Course Enrollment & Policies

You can register directly at our center or contact us to schedule a consultation. Once you've decided on the course, we'll assist you with the registration process.

Course changes can be made within the first week of the course, depending on availability. Please contact us directly to discuss your options.

If you wish to cancel your enrollment, please inform us within the first 7 days. Refunds are subject to our cancellation policy.

Transfers are not permitted. However, you may cancel and re-enroll in a future batch, subject to availability.

If you miss a class, you can catch up by attending a makeup session (if available) or by reviewing the materials shared by the instructor.

Yes, depending on the course and availability, we offer the option to extend your enrollment. Please speak with our staff for more details.

Our courses are flexible, and we understand that life happens. If you need more time, we can help you find a solution, such as extending your enrollment.

We offer a 7-day refund policy if you're not satisfied with the course content. Please review our refund policy for the full details.

Yes, you can take a break from your course, but please inform us about the duration and ensure your seat is reserved when you return.

Any changes to your registration must be done within the first week of the course. Please reach out to our support team for assistance.
