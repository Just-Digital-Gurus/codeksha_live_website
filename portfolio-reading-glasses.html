Screen Time and Eye Care Tips for Developers

February 1, 2024 Codeksha

Long hours in front of a screen come with the territory of learning and working in web development. A few practical habits can make a real difference to comfort over time.

The 20-20-20 Habit

A commonly recommended habit is looking at something roughly 20 feet away for 20 seconds every 20 minutes, which gives eye muscles a brief break from close-up screen focus.

Screen Brightness and Room Lighting

Matching your screen brightness to the lighting in the room (rather than a very bright screen in a dark room, or vice versa) reduces eye strain over long sessions.

Text Size and Font Choice While Coding

Using a comfortable font size and a readable coding font in your editor - rather than squinting at small default text - matters more than it seems over hours of daily use.

Small habits like these add up over a 24-week course - our instructors encourage sustainable study habits, not just long, unbroken hours.
