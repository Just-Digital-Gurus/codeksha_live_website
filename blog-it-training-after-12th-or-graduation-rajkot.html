IT Training After 12th or Graduation in Rajkot: What to Consider

December 18, 2025 6 min read Codeksha Team

Whether you've just finished 12th grade or recently graduated, deciding what IT training to pursue in Rajkot is a genuinely important decision. Here's what's worth thinking through before choosing.

You Don't Need a Computer Science Degree to Start

Many working developers come from non-CS academic backgrounds. Structured, practical IT training can be a genuine entry point into tech regardless of your specific 12th-grade stream or degree subject.

Decide Between Breadth (Full Stack) and Depth (a Specific Track)

Students right after 12th or graduation often benefit from a broad starting point, like Full Stack Development, since it exposes you to more of the field before you specialize. Others with a clearer direction may prefer going straight into a focused track like Frontend Design, Backend Programming, or Digital Marketing.

Ask About Real Project Exposure, Not Just Certificates

At this stage, what you can actually demonstrate (real projects, a working portfolio) matters more for getting hired than the certificate alone - ask any institute directly about the project work included in a course.

Consider Whether Classroom or Self-Study Fits Your Discipline Level

Structured, in-person classroom training can provide the accountability and schedule that many students, especially early in their careers, benefit from compared to fully self-directed online learning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which course should I choose right after 12th grade?

It depends on your interests - Full Stack Development is a reasonable broad starting point if you're unsure, while more focused tracks suit students with a clearer direction already.
