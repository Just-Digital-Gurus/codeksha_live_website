Presentations

Empowering Education

March 15, 2024 Codeksha

There's a meaningful difference between being taught by someone who has studied a subject academically and being taught by someone who actively practices it for a living. Both have value, but they teach differently.

Teaching From Current Practice

A working developer teaching a course draws on patterns and decisions from real, recent projects - not just a fixed curriculum written years ago and left unchanged.

Answering 'Why' From Experience

When a student asks why something is done a certain way, an instructor with real project experience can usually answer from direct experience, including the mistakes that led to learning it - not just from a textbook explanation.

Modeling Real Problem-Solving, Not Just Final Answers

Watching a working developer debug a real problem live - including the dead ends - teaches a kind of problem-solving that a clean, pre-written example rarely can.

This is exactly how Codeksha's instructors teach - working developers from Creatiz and Just Digital Gurus, not disconnected lecturers.
